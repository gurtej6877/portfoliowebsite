About Me ✨

Business, creativity, and problem solving, these things keep me going ( with a strong cup of coffee ☕ ). As a commerce student focused on finance, strategy, and management, I’m passionate about linking numbers with innovation to create real impact. 📈

My entrepreneurial journey began with IndiGuide, where I built skills in leadership, adaptability, and strategic decision-making. I also have a strong interest in technology from networking and Linux to Generative AI. 💻

Outside business, I’m often behind the camera, capturing moments and refining edits. Photography has taught me perspective, storytelling, and how to shape experiences—skills I applied while judging and managing school photography competitions. 📸

Right now, I’m building expertise in finance, business, and management, exploring GenAI applications, and working to make complex concepts simple. Long-term, I’m aiming for an MBA at a top-tier school—whether IIM, Harvard, or INSEAD—and a career in Finance. 🎓

If you’re working on something meaningful in finance, business, or innovation, I’d love to connect. I’m here for genuine conversations, not just brief greetings and disappearances. 🚀
